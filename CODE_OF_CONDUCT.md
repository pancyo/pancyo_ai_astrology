## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sharing another person's private information without permission, or other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private contact method. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or take other appropriate action to protect the community.
